Project Overview

This project is a Power BI Dashboard created to analyze Netflix stock price trends over different dates.
The goal is to visually explore high & low stock prices, compare opening prices, and identify dates with highest & lowest stock movements.

🔹 Key Features

Top & Bottom Analysis

Top 5 dates with highest stock price

Top 5 dates with lowest stock price

Price Comparisons

High vs Low stock price trend by date

Highest & Lowest stock price compared with Opening price

Interactive Visuals

Filters and slicers to explore data by date ranges

Dynamic visuals (bar chart, line chart) for easy understanding

🔹 Tools & Technologies

Power BI Desktop for data modeling and dashboard design

Excel/CSV Data Source containing Netflix stock price history

DAX Measures for calculating Sum of High, Sum of Low, and comparisons

🔹 Data Insights

Identified key dates when Netflix stock reached peak values and bottom values.

Showed clear trends of stock fluctuations over a selected time period.

🔹 Workflow

Data Import – Loaded CSV/Excel stock data into Power BI.

Data Cleaning – Checked data types, removed nulls.

Data Modeling – Created relationships and measures for High, Low, and Open price.

Visualization – Designed interactive charts and tables with a dark theme.
